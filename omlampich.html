Perfect 10: Gymnastics favorites secure their spot in the team event finals

By Sports Desk | Updated July 2026

PARIS — The competition venue reached a fever pitch last night as the favorites delivered a masterclass performance, easily locking down their position for the upcoming team event finals. Executing high-difficulty routines with seamless precision, the squad invoked the spirit of historic "Perfect 10" milestones.

While the modern scoring system has evolved past the traditional flat 10.0 cap, the sheer execution on display left both the judges and the crowd stunned, setting up what promises to be an unforgettable gold-medal showdown.

Olympic Gymnastics Quick Facts

  • The First "10": 14-year-old Romanian gymnast Nadia Comăneci achieved the first-ever perfect 10.00 at the 1976 Montreal Games.
  • The Scoreboard Glitch: In 1976, scoreboards weren't programmed for 4 digits, so Nadia's historic perfect score actually flashed as "1.00".
  • Modern System: In 2006, the system changed to combine a Difficulty Score (D-score) with an Execution Score (E-score) starting at 10.
